***Play Piano with your computer's keyboard***

One octave piano interface with 11 notes from C to B.
You may change octaves using X for higher pitch and Z for lower. 
Use Space for pressing your sustain pedal and keep the sound even if you are not pressing. 
Adjust the volume with the volume knob on the left. 

*Demo available at: https://pianomidijuan.netlify.app*
